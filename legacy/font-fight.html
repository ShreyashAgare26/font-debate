How stats are measured

Each font is set at 100px on a hidden canvas in your browser. Raw values are then scaled 40 to 100 across the roster.

HP
Ink coverage. Share of the box filled by “Hamburgefonstiv”. Heavier type is tougher.
ATK
x-height in em. Big lowercase hits harder at small sizes.
DEF
Cap height in em. Tall capitals hold the line.
SPD
Narrowness (1 ÷ average letter width) plus the kerning on “AV”. Tight type moves first.

Class chart

Row attacks column. ×1.5 is a strong matchup, ×0.67 a weak one.
